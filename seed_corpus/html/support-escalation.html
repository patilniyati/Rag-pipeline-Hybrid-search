Support Escalation Matrix

Classify incidents by customer impact, not by how difficult the bug looks to debug. The incident lead can raise severity when new evidence shows wider impact.

Severity levels

LevelDefinitionInitial action
Sev-1Broad production outage, suspected customer-data exposure, or widespread inability to submit jobsPage incident lead and security owner when applicable; post first update within 15 minutes
Sev-2Major degradation affecting multiple workspaces with a workaround availablePage API on-call and update affected teams within 30 minutes
Sev-3Limited impact, isolated workspace, or non-critical feature failureCreate a tracked ticket and provide the next update within one business day
Sev-4Question, documentation issue, or cosmetic defect without service impactHandle through normal support queue

Evidence checklist

Include UTC time range, request ID, route, workspace identifier, response status, affected regions, and what has already been tried. Remove credentials and unnecessary personal data. For INC-204-like latency, include p95, 5xx rate, database connection wait, and oldest dispatch-message age.
