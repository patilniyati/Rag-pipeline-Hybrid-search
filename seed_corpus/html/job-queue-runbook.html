Job Queue Runbook

Use this runbook when accepted jobs are delayed, repeatedly failing, or visible in the dead-letter queue (DLQ).

Queue names

dispatch contains accepted work waiting for workers. archive stores completed job metadata for retrieval. notify carries lifecycle events to configured webhook endpoints.

DLQ-17

Error code DLQ-17 indicates that a job exceeded eight processing attempts and was moved to the dead-letter queue. This does not by itself prove that the downstream side effect failed; inspect the attempt history and downstream system before retrying.

Safe redrive

  1. Inspect the final error and attempt timestamps.
  2. Confirm that the downstream operation is no longer in progress and determine whether it already completed.
  3. Fix the underlying cause or document why the failure is transient.
  4. Use relay-admin jobs redrive --job-id JOB_ID --preserve-idempotency.
  5. Verify the job reaches a terminal state and watch for a second DLQ-17 event.

The --preserve-idempotency flag is mandatory for redrives of jobs that can cause external side effects. Reporting identities do not have permission to redrive; an on-call operator must perform the action.

Queue alerts

Investigate when the oldest dispatch message is more than 180 seconds old for five minutes, or when DLQ-17 exceeds 25 occurrences in ten minutes. Compare worker saturation and downstream dependency health before increasing worker count.
