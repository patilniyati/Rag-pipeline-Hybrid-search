NORTHSTAR RELAY RELEASE NOTES
Release window: September 2026

2026-09-22 - Webhook diagnostics
Added event ID, delivery attempt, response class, and correlation value to the delivery log. Signing secrets and raw payload bodies remain redacted. Retry behavior remains up to six attempts over a 24-hour window.

2026-09-10 - Multipart exports
Exports larger than 5 GB are split into numbered parts. A manifest provides a checksum for each part. Download all parts and verify the checksums before reassembly. Retrying a failed export creates a new export job ID; the old download URL is not resumed.

2026-09-03 - Workspace reporting role
A reporting identity can read job status and export metadata but cannot redrive dead-letter jobs or change workspace permissions. Redrive remains an on-call operator action.

2026-08-28 - Queue observability
Added oldest dispatch-message age and DLQ-17 volume to the operator dashboard. Investigation thresholds are documented in the Reliability SLO and Alerts policy.

Compatibility note: these release notes do not supersede the current security, retention, or reliability policies.
