WORKSPACE LIMITS FAQ

Q: Is the POST /v2/jobs limit per API key?
A: No. The standard limit is 120 requests per minute per workspace, with a burst allowance of 20. Multiple keys in one workspace share the same limit.

Q: What should a client do after HTTP 429?
A: Honor Retry-After, then retry with exponential backoff and jitter. Do not immediately replay the request in a tight loop.

Q: How many jobs can be active in one workspace?
A: The standard active-job cap is 2,500. Completed jobs do not count toward the active-job cap, though their metadata remains subject to its own retention period.

Q: What is the maximum size for a single export file before multipart packaging?
A: Exports larger than 5 GB are packaged as numbered parts with a manifest and per-part checksums.

Q: Who can re-drive a dead-letter job?
A: An on-call operator. A reporting identity can read status and export metadata but cannot redrive jobs.

Q: When is the daily export cutoff?
A: 02:00 UTC. Records created at or after that cutoff are eligible for the following daily export.

Q: How long is an API key valid?
A: Production keys should be rotated every 90 days. After rotation, the former key has a 24-hour overlap window, after which it should be revoked. This is distinct from the 24-hour idempotency-key retention period.
